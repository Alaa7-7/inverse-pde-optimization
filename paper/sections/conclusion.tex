\section{Conclusion}

This study presented a genetic algorithm-based framework for solving inverse problems in the advection-diffusion equation.

The numerical experiments demonstrate that the proposed method can successfully recover unknown physical parameters with high accuracy:

\[
(v, D) \approx (0.7804, 0.0508)
\]

compared to the true values:

\[
(v, D) = (0.8, 0.05)
\]

The low estimation error and stable convergence confirm the effectiveness of combining numerical PDE solvers with evolutionary optimization techniques.

Future work will focus on improving convergence speed and extending the method to higher-dimensional PDE systems.
