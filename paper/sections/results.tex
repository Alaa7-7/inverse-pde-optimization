\section{Results}

The proposed method was evaluated using synthetic data generated with known ground truth parameters:

\[
v_{true} = 0.8, \quad D_{true} = 0.05
\]

After running multiple stochastic optimization trials, the following results were obtained:

\subsection{Parameter Estimation}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Estimated velocity (mean):
    \[
    v_{est} = 0.7804
    \]

    \item Estimated diffusion coefficient (mean):
    \[
    D_{est} = 0.0508
    \]
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Error Analysis}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Mean absolute error in velocity:
    \[
    E_v = 0.0236
    \]

    \item Mean absolute error in diffusion coefficient:
    \[
    E_D = 0.0020
    \]
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Statistical Stability}

The standard deviation over multiple runs indicates stable convergence:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Std of velocity estimates: $0.0266$
    \item Std of diffusion estimates: $0.0013$
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Key Observation}

The results demonstrate that the proposed genetic algorithm-based inverse solver is able to accurately recover the unknown parameters even in the presence of noise, with low estimation error and stable statistical behavior.
