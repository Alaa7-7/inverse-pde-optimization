\section{Methodology}

\subsection{Forward Model}
The forward problem is solved using a finite difference scheme with explicit time stepping. Spatial derivatives are approximated using upwind and central difference schemes for advection and diffusion terms respectively.

\subsection{Inverse Problem Formulation}
Given noisy observations $u_{obs}$, the goal is to minimize the cost function:

\[
J(v, D) = \| u_{sim}(v, D) - u_{obs} \|_2^2
\]

\subsection{Optimization Method}
A genetic algorithm is used to solve the inverse optimization problem. The algorithm evolves a population of candidate solutions through selection, crossover, and mutation to minimize the cost function.
