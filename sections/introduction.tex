\section{Introduction}

Inverse problems for partial differential equations (PDEs) are fundamental in computational physics and engineering, where the objective is to estimate unknown physical parameters from observed data.

In this work, we consider the one-dimensional advection-diffusion equation, which models transport phenomena in fluid dynamics:

\[
\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} + v \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} = D \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}
\]

where $u(x,t)$ is the transported quantity, $v$ is the advection velocity, and $D$ is the diffusion coefficient.

The main objective of this study is to recover the unknown parameters $(v, D)$ from noisy observations using an optimization-based inverse modeling approach.
